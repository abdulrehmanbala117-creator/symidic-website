Our Process

From discovery to AI-powered support.

A structured 5-step process that takes your Shopify store from inquiry to fully operational AI customer support and automation — with clarity at every stage.

Step 01

Discovery Call

We start with a free discovery conversation — learning about your Shopify store, your customer support challenges, your product catalog, existing workflows and your goals. No sales pitch, just understanding.

Step 02

Strategy & Setup

Based on the discovery call, we define the scope of your AI support agent — integration points, knowledge base structure, brand voice configuration and human handoff rules. You get a clear plan and timeline before we build anything.

Step 03

Build & Integrate

Our team builds your custom AI agent and automation flows — connecting to your Shopify store's product data, orders, FAQs and support policies. Every response is trained on your actual business content.

Step 04

Test & Launch

Before going live, we thoroughly test the agent with real scenarios — product questions, order inquiries, edge cases and handoff flows. Once validated, we launch on your store.

Step 05

Optimize & Support

Post-launch, we monitor performance, review analytics and continuously optimize. As your store evolves — new products, updated policies, seasonal changes — your AI support evolves with it.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does the full process take?

Timelines depend on scope and complexity. During the discovery call, we provide a tailored estimate based on your specific requirements, integrations and workflow needs.

Do I need to provide product data manually?

No. During the build phase, we integrate directly with your Shopify store to access product catalog data, order information and existing support content.

Can I add new features after launch?

Absolutely. Post-launch optimization is part of our process. You can expand knowledge base content, add automations and configure additional workflows.

What if I need changes after launch?

SYMIDIC provides ongoing support and optimization. As your store evolves — new products, updated policies, seasonal needs — your AI support evolves with it.
