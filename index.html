The AI pipeline

How Every Customer Message Is Handled In Milliseconds

Every customer message flows through an intelligent multi-agent pipeline — understanding intent, retrieving real-time data from your store, and delivering an instant, accurate answer.

Stage 01 // Ingestion

Omnichannel Customer Ingestion

Inquiries arrive via Shopify Inbox, WhatsApp, Gorgias, Zendesk, or email. The agent ingests context with zero queue latency.

Stage 02 // Neural Intent

Multi-Agent Intent & Policy Reasoning

The neural engine classifies the exact intent (Order Tracking, Exchange, Product Sizing, Refund Request) and checks warranty rules.

Stage 03 // Deep Store Sync

Real-Time Shopify GraphQL Querying

Queries live store inventory, courier fulfillment tracking APIs, and customer order history securely with zero guessing.

Stage 04 // Resolution

Instant Resolution or VIP Human Handoff

Customer receives instant resolution with tracking link or label. Sensitive edge cases route directly to your human staff with full summaries.
