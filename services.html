Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What does a SYMIDIC AI customer support agent handle?

FAQs, product information, order tracking, shipping and delivery questions, and returns/refunds guidance — powered by your store's data and policies, with automatic handoff to your team for complex or sensitive conversations.

Will the AI agent know my products and policies?

Yes. During setup, the agent is integrated with your Shopify product catalog, order data, FAQs and support policies, so every response reflects your actual business content.

What happens when the AI can't answer a question?

The AI agent seamlessly hands off to a human on your team. You define the triggers — topic, complexity, sentiment or other criteria.

Do I need technical knowledge to get started?

No. SYMIDIC handles the build, integration and launch. You define your requirements during the discovery call and we take care of the rest.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing is tailored to your store's scope — complexity, integrations and usage. You'll receive a clear proposal after the discovery call.
